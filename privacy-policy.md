# Privacy Policy for Settle: Group Expenses

**Last updated: June 2026**

## Overview

Settle: Group Expenses ("Settle", "we", "us") is a mobile application that helps groups of people split and track shared expenses. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

**Account Information**
When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name. You may optionally provide a username and PayPal username.

**Profile Photo**
If you choose to upload a profile photo, it is stored securely on our servers.

**Expense Data**
We store the events, expenses, and payment records you create within the app. This includes expense names, amounts, and the names of people involved in each expense.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Create and manage your account
- Display your profile to other users you share events with
- Calculate and track shared expenses
- Enable payment coordination between users

## Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your display name and username are visible to other users you share events with. Your email address is shared with other users within your events solely to facilitate e-Transfer payments.

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a third-party database provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## Third-Party Services

Settle integrates with the following third-party services:

- **Supabase** - Database and authentication
- **PayPal** - Optional payment links (we do not process payments directly)

## Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below.

## Children's Privacy

Settle is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes.
