# Privacy Policy for Settle: Group Expenses

**Last updated: November 2026**

## Overview

Settle: Group Expenses ("Settle", "we", "us") is a mobile app that helps groups of people split and track shared expenses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal information.

## Information We Collect

**Account information**
When you create an account, we collect your email address and display name. You also choose a username, and you may optionally add a PayPal username.

**Profile photo**
If you upload a profile photo, it is stored on our servers and shown next to your name in the app.

**Events, expenses and payments**
We store the events you create or join, the people in them, and the expenses and payments recorded in them. This includes each expense's name, amount, category, date, any note you add, who paid, and how it is split.

**Receipt photos**
You can choose to attach a photo of a receipt to an expense, either by taking a picture or picking one from your photo library. The photo is uploaded to our servers and stored with that expense.

**Friends**
We store your friend requests and your list of friends.

**Activity**
We keep a record of actions in your events, such as an expense being added, a payment being marked as paid, or someone being added to an event. We also record friend requests. This is used to show your activity feed.

**Push notification token**
If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can send you notifications. The token identifies your device only for delivering notifications.

**Camera and photo library**
Settle only uses your camera or photo library when you choose to take or attach a photo, either a receipt or a profile photo. We never access your photos in the background.

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Calculate and track shared expenses and balances
- Show you and the people you share events with who paid, who owes, and what has been settled
- Let people find you and add you as a friend
- Show your activity feed and send you notifications
- Help you pay each other back (for example by showing an email for e-Transfer or a PayPal link)

## Who Can See Your Information

- **People in your events** can see your display name, username, profile photo, and the expenses, notes, receipt photos and payments in those events. Your email address is shown to people in your events so they can send you an e-Transfer.
- **Anyone signed in to Settle** can search for you by username. Search results show your display name, username and profile photo, so they can send you a friend request.
- **Your friends** can see your display name, username and profile photo, and can add you to events.
- **Anyone with an event's invite link or code** can join that event. Once they join, they can see its members and expenses like any other member. Only share invite links with people you want in the event.

## Push Notifications

If you turn on notifications, Settle may notify you when an expense or payment is added to one of your events, when you're added to an event, or when you get a friend request. Notifications can include names and amounts.

Notifications are sent through Expo's push notification service and Apple Push Notification service. You can turn them off at any time from the Profile tab in Settle or in your phone's Settings.

## Data Storage

Your data is stored using Supabase, a third-party database and storage provider. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## Third-Party Services

Settle uses these services to run:

- **Supabase**: database, sign-in and file storage (including profile and receipt photos)
- **Expo**: delivering push notifications
- **Apple Push Notification service**: delivering push notifications to iPhones
- **PayPal**: optional payment links. We do not process payments or see your payment details.

## Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time from the Profile tab in Settle by tapping **Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account, your profile, and the expenses and payments tied to you. Events that only you were part of are deleted completely. Events that other people are still in stay available to them.

If you'd like anything else removed, or can't access the app, email us at the address below and we'll take care of it.

## Children's Privacy

Settle is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
